Ai7 interface 0.4.0

1. Drag Ai7 interface into Applications.
2. Connect a USB data cable, turn on the recorder and stop recording.
3. Close MindRec on phone and Mac. Open Ai7 interface and allow Bluetooth access.
4. Scan, select your recorder and Connect. The app fetches its list and enables USB mode.
5. Use Settings (gear icon) to choose your local destination folder.
6. Once USB is ready, transfer selected recordings or all new recordings.
7. Eject NO NAME in Finder before unplugging.

All audio is copied through USB and converted locally to MP3. Bluetooth is used for setup.
If the disk is not found, check the cable and choose the USB volume manually in Settings.
Notes, Calls and preserved Originals are grouped per recorder in your selected folder.
The new label means no final local files exist. Local means hashes were verified.
Unverified, incomplete or conflicting files are never overwritten automatically.
Device deletion is not available in this USB workflow. Disconnect does not eject the disk.
The full automatic USB workflow still needs live hardware validation.

macOS 11 or newer. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. The Intel slice is compiled
but has not been tested on a physical Intel Mac. Tested recorder firmware: V1.0.0.
There is no guaranteed compatibility with every recorder sold as Ai7 / AI07.

This preview is ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may block
an Internet-downloaded copy. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other security protections.
For trusted distribution without this warning, the publisher must sign and notarize a
future release with an Apple Developer account.

No Homebrew or extra audio tools are required. FFmpeg and LAME are bundled; their licenses,
corresponding source archives and rebuild recipe are in Third-party sources.

GitHub project: https://github.com/jorndevreede/Ai7-MacOs-Export
