Ai7 interface 0.2.0

1. Drag Ai7 interface into Applications.
2. Open the app, and allow Bluetooth access when macOS asks.
3. Choose a local folder. Notes, Calls and preserved Originals are grouped per recorder.
4. Turn on the Ai7 and close MindRec on both phone and Mac.
5. Click Scan, select the recorder, and click Connect.
6. Check recordings and click Transfer selected, or click Transfer all new recordings.

The new label means no final local files exist in this device's export folder.
Local means the source size and both audio hashes have been verified.
Local (unverified), incomplete or conflict are never overwritten automatically.

Delete selected from Ai7 permanently deletes only the checked recordings after an explicit
confirmation. The app checks a fresh recorder list after each deletion. Your local files
are kept. Cancel is the default button. Transfer important files first.

macOS 11 or newer. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. The Intel slice is compiled
but has not been tested on a physical Intel Mac. Tested recorder firmware: V1.0.0.
There is no guaranteed compatibility with every recorder sold as Ai7 / AI07.

This preview is ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may block
an Internet-downloaded copy. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other security protections.
For trusted distribution without this warning, the publisher must sign and notarize a
future release with an Apple Developer account.

No Homebrew or extra audio tools are required. FFmpeg and LAME are bundled; their licenses,
corresponding source archives and rebuild recipe are in Third-party sources.

GitHub project: https://github.com/jorndevreede/ai07-macos-export
